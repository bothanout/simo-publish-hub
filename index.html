Simo Publish Hub

A creator productivity tool for publishing original short-form video.

What it does

Simo Publish Hub helps creators publish their own original short-form videos to their own TikTok account. It uploads videos, and lets the creator draft and schedule posts — all content is original and owned by the creator.

How it works

  1. The creator connects their own TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit (OAuth).
  2. The creator selects one of their own finished videos and writes a caption.
  3. Simo Publish Hub uploads the video through TikTok's official Content Posting API and publishes or schedules it on the creator's own account.

Videos are only ever posted to the account that authorized the app. Nothing is posted without the creator's explicit action.

Data & privacy

The app requests only the minimum TikTok permissions needed: basic profile info (to identify the connected account), video upload, and video publish. Details are in the Privacy Policy. Use of the tool is governed by the Terms of Service.
