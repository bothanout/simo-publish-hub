Privacy Policy — Simo Publish Hub

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. What this tool is

Simo Publish Hub ("the Tool") is a creator productivity tool that lets a creator upload their own original short-form videos to their own TikTok account and draft or schedule posts, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

2. Data we access

When you connect your TikTok account, the Tool accesses only what is needed to publish your videos, under the TikTok permissions you approve:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic) — to identify which TikTok account you connected (open ID, display name).
  • Video upload (video.upload) — to upload the video files you choose to publish.
  • Video publish (video.publish) — to publish or schedule those videos on your own account.

The Tool does not read your messages, followers, likes, comments, or any other TikTok data.

3. How your data is used

  • Your TikTok access tokens are stored securely on your own machine and used only to upload and publish videos you explicitly choose.
  • Your videos and captions are sent to TikTok only when you publish or schedule a post.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or analytics.

4. Data retention

Access tokens are kept on your device until you disconnect the Tool or revoke access. Videos you upload are processed by TikTok under TikTok's Privacy Policy.

5. Revoking access / deleting data

You can revoke the Tool's access at any time from your TikTok settings (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). To request deletion of any data the Tool holds about you, email bothanout101@gmail.com and it will be deleted within 30 days.

6. Children's privacy

The Tool is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact
